✦ About MR.Career

A clearer path from career goal to practical progress.

MR.Career is a browser-based workspace for exploring IT careers, understanding what to learn for a target role, organising study, practising skills, building projects and preparing for interviews. It brings these steps together so you can focus on the next useful action.

What you can do with MR.Career

The workspace connects career exploration, guided learning and practical job preparation. Use the tools that support your current goal.

⌕

Discover and compare careers

Explore technology paths, compare role expectations and select a target role to guide your learning.

▤

Follow guided lessons

Use role-based topics with plain-language concepts, worked examples, practical steps, common mistakes and key takeaways.

▦

Build a dated study plan

Organise learning into calendar sessions, mark work complete and move missed sessions into catch-up.

⚒

Practise and build projects

Work through practical labs and project checklists that help turn learning into demonstrable work.

◎

Prepare for interviews

Use role-focused prompts and answer outlines to practise explaining concepts and your approach.

↗

Review résumé and job descriptions

Analyse pasted résumé text for role-related skill coverage and inspect skill areas found in a job description.

✓

Review readiness and progress

Use learning, lab and interview practice signals to identify areas to revisit and keep next actions visible.

✦

Find certification guidance

Access certification and exam guidance alongside your roadmap and hands-on practice.

⇧

Manage your own workspace data

Save progress in your browser and export or import a local backup when you want to keep or restore it.

Designed around the next useful step

You do not need to configure everything at once. Start with a direction, then use the relevant tools as you progress.

STEP 01

Choose a role or goal

Explore careers or choose a target role so the roadmap and preparation tools have a clear direction.

STEP 02

Understand and plan

Read the learning concepts and examples, then organise selected topics into a study schedule that suits you.

STEP 03

Practise and improve

Use labs, projects, interview preparation and progress reviews to decide what to practise next.

Your workspace, on your device.

MR.Career is designed as a local-first static application. Progress is stored in your browser, not automatically synced across devices. Export a backup before clearing browser data or switching browsers.

What MR.Career is — and isn't

MR.Career is a learning and planning aid. It helps organise preparation; it does not replace hands-on experience or independent verification.

✓

A practical organiser

Use it to structure learning, record progress, practise skills and prepare for conversations about your work.

↗

Not a hiring guarantee

Readiness indicators and résumé or job-description analysis are guidance, not an employer decision or a guarantee of interviews, jobs, salary or certification results.

⌑

Verify important details

Check certification requirements, exam codes, costs and job requirements with the official provider or employer before making decisions.
