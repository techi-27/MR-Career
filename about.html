About MR.Career

MR.Career is a browser-based learning and career-planning workspace for exploring IT roles, planning study, practising skills, tracking projects and preparing for interviews.

The app is designed to work without an account or server-side database. Learning data is stored locally in your browser. Use the Export feature to keep a backup before clearing browser data or changing devices.

Educational purpose

Content is intended for general educational and career-planning purposes. It is not a guarantee of employment, salary, certification success or hiring outcomes. Always verify certification details and job requirements with the relevant provider or employer.
