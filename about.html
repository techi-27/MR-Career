✦ About MR.Career

Build your next career move with more clarity.

⌑

Designed with local-first privacy in mind

MR.Career is designed to work without an account or server-side database. Workspace data is stored in your browser on the device you use. It is not automatically synced across browsers or devices. Export a backup before clearing browser data or switching devices.

Educational purpose

MR.Career provides general educational and career-planning information. It does not guarantee employment, salary, certification success, or hiring outcomes. Verify certification details and job requirements with the relevant provider or employer.
