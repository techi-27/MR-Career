Privacy

MR.Career is designed as a local-first static website. The application stores learning progress and preferences in browser storage on the device and browser profile you use. The site owner does not receive that local data through the app's current local-only features.

Your choices

  • Use Export to save a backup file; keep it private because it may contain career notes and progress.
  • Use Import to restore a backup. Importing replaces current app data, so export current progress first if needed.
  • Use Reset only when you intend to remove local application data.
  • Browser storage may be removed by clearing site data, using private browsing, or changing browsers or devices.

Hosting and external links

The hosting provider may process standard technical information such as requests and server logs under its own policies. Links to third-party sites are governed by those sites' privacy practices.

If analytics, advertising, forms or other data-collecting services are added in the future, this notice should be updated before they are enabled.

This page provides general information, not legal advice. Review it against your actual hosting configuration and applicable requirements before launch.

Read the Terms of Use or contact the site owner.

Your data stays local

Current app planning data is stored in your browser. Export a backup if you need to move it or keep a copy.
